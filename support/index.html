Support Luna Terps

Luna Terps is a team of University of Maryland mechatronics engineering students building a lunar construction robot for NASA’s Lunabotics Challenge. Sponsors, donors, and mentors make the hardware, testing, and travel possible.

Three ways to help

  • Sponsor the robot

    Companies and organizations

    Fund a subsystem or the season as a whole, and follow the engineering as it develops.

    • A named place in the sponsor listing once you approve it
    • Progress updates through the season
    • A route to meet students working across every discipline

  • Make a gift

    Alumni, families, and friends

    Gifts of any size go toward parts, testing, and getting the team to the runs in Florida.

    • Every gift goes to the season's build and travel
    • Support for students learning to build and operate a robot

  • Give parts, time, or expertise

    Engineers, shops, and mentors

    Components, machining time, test space, and design reviews matter as much as funding.

    • Donated parts and services credited like any other support
    • Mentors invited to design reviews and test days

Where support goes

Parts that survive abrasive simulant, testing that looks like the arena, and getting the robot and team to Florida in May.

  • Robot hardware

    Motors, controllers, batteries, structure, and wear parts for a robot of up to 80 kg.

  • Sensing and compute

    Depth cameras, lidar, and the onboard computer that runs perception and autonomy.

  • Testing

    Regolith simulant, fixtures, and practice space that behaves like the competition arena.

  • Travel to Florida

    Bringing the robot and the on-site team to the runs near Kennedy Space Center in May 2027.
