Join Luna Terps

Luna Terps is a team of University of Maryland mechatronics engineering students building a robot for NASA’s Lunabotics Challenge. This page explains who can join and the roles members take on.

Who can join

Membership is open to students in the University of Maryland mechatronics engineering program.

The robot draws on the whole program: mechanical design, electronics, embedded control, and autonomy software, plus the systems engineering papers that carry nearly half of the grand-prize score.

Not in the program?

Engineers, machine shops, and mentors can still help through parts, machining time, test space, and design reviews.

Ways to support the team

Where your skills fit

Six roles cover the robot and the season’s papers. Most real tasks cross more than one.

On the robot

Mechanical
Chassis, drivetrain, excavation, and deposition hardware that keeps working in fine, abrasive simulant, inside an 80 kg limit.
Electrical
Batteries, power distribution, motor controllers, wiring, and the emergency stop.
Embedded and controls
Microcontroller firmware, sensor interfaces, and the control loops that turn commands into motion.
Autonomy software
Perception, localization, path planning, and dig-and-deposit cycles that run with as little operator input as possible.

Around the robot

Systems engineering
Requirements, trade studies, budgets, and the Project Management Plan, design review, and Systems Engineering Paper that carry 35 of the 75 grand-prize points.
Operations and testing
Test procedures, safety, run planning, and the mission-control roles that operate the robot from outside the arena.
