The challenge

Dig crushed basalt simulant, carry it past boulders and craters, and pile it into a berm inside a marked area.

The robot can weigh at most 80 kg and is driven from a control room outside the arena, or runs on its own for extra points. A berm like this could shield a lunar landing site from rocket blast.
